    Reading notes

    • European NATO = 29 Allies excluding the United States and Canada.
    • 2024–2025 are NATO estimates (marked e).
    • Absolute category amounts = total × category share ÷ 100.
    • Germany 2025 NATO national-currency, USD, and GDP-share cells were unavailable at the 3 June 2025 cut-off.
    • Personnel: compare active (IISS full-time) only; reserve/paramilitary are secondary.
    • Spending dataset accessed 10 October 2026; personnel 11 October 2026.
